## The Problem We Solve

Search has changed. When your buyers type a question into Google today, they often get a paragraph answer — written by AI — before they ever see a list of websites. That AI answer cites specific sources. If your brand isn't one of them, the conversation moves on without you, and you never know it happened.

Traditional rank tracking tools count your position in the blue links. They don't read the AI answer. They don't tell you who got cited. They don't explain why you were skipped. **VSI does all three** — and then tells your team exactly what to do about it.

## What VSI Does, Start to Finish

The platform runs a complete loop: **Track → Diagnose → Plan → Execute → Verify.**

Every capability in the platform exists to move one keyword from "AI isn't mentioning us" to "we're the first thing cited."

## Capability 1 — Visibility Tracking

**What you see:** A live, up-to-date picture of where your brand stands — both in traditional Google results and inside AI-generated answers.

### What VSI tracks for every keyword you care about:

| Signal | What It Tells You |
|---|---|
| **Google ranking** | Your position in the top 10 results, the page title, and the URL that's ranking |
| **AI Mode presence** | Whether Google shows an AI-written answer for this search at all |
| **AI Mode citation** | Whether your brand's website is listed as a source inside that AI answer |
| **Brand mention in AI text** | Whether your brand name appears in the AI's written answer, even without a direct link |
| **AI Overview** | Tracks a second, newer AI answer surface that Google shows separately — your citation status there too |
| **ChatGPT visibility** | Whether ChatGPT cites or mentions your brand when someone asks it the same question |
| **Competitor citations** | Every other website the AI chose to cite instead of you — with their position, page title, and URL |
| **Gap classification** | Each keyword gets a clear status: Winning, Mentioned, AI-Invisible, or Not Capturing |

### What "gap classification" means in plain terms:

- 🟢 **Winning** — You rank well on Google *and* AI cites you. This is the goal.
- 🔵 **Mentioned** — Your brand name appears in the AI answer, but you're not a cited source. Close, but incomplete.
- 🟡 **AI-Invisible** — You rank well on Google, but the AI is ignoring you completely. Your traffic is at risk.
- 🔴 **Not Capturing** — You're neither ranking nor cited. This is where competitors are taking clicks that should be yours.

**Why it matters:** You can't fix what you can't see. VSI gives you a single, honest number for every keyword — not a screenshot, not a guess, but a live data point with a timestamped record of what the AI actually said.

## Capability 2 — AI Diagnosis

**What you get:** A specific, written explanation for every underperforming keyword — not just "improve your content," but the actual reason the AI is skipping you.

### Per-keyword diagnosis includes:

- **Current situation summary** — Where you rank, whether AI Mode is active, and what the AI said about your category
- **Why AI skipped your page** — A plain-language explanation of what the cited competitors have that your page doesn't (depth, structure, authority signals, entity coverage, freshness)
- **Who's winning and why** — The specific domains the AI is citing and the reason they're being chosen over you
- **Topic match check** — Sometimes the AI answer is actually about a different topic than you'd expect. VSI flags this so your team doesn't waste effort optimising for the wrong thing
- **Confidence score** — Every diagnosis tells you how reliable it is, based on how fresh and complete the underlying data is

**Why it matters:** Most agencies spend weeks doing this manually — reading AI answers one by one, comparing competitor pages by hand. VSI produces this for every keyword in your account automatically, so your strategist walks in on Monday already knowing the answer.

## Capability 3 — Citation Strategy

**What you get:** After diagnosing the gap, VSI goes one step further: it reads the actual pages that competitors are using to get cited, extracts the patterns that make them citation-worthy, and tells you specifically what your page needs to match or beat them.

### The citation analysis includes:

- **What competitors are doing right** — The content patterns, source depth, structure choices, and authority signals that appear consistently across the pages the AI is citing
- **What your page is missing** — A direct comparison between the competitor pages and your ranking page, with specific gaps named
- **Your page's strengths** — What you already have that's working, so your team doesn't undo something that's already helping
- **Specific page changes** — Not vague suggestions, but exact edits: which sections to expand, what structure to add, which sources to cite
- **A prioritised action plan** — Every recommendation comes with an effort estimate (hours vs. days) and an impact rating, so your team can sequence the work sensibly

**Why it matters:** Citation analysis used to require an SEO specialist spending hours manually reading and comparing pages. VSI does this automatically for any keyword on demand, and the output is already formatted as a brief your writers can act on immediately.

## Capability 4 — Execution & Task Tracking

**What you get:** A task management system built specifically for search and AI visibility work — where every task is tied to the keyword data that created it, and the platform checks whether completing the task actually moved the needle.

### How tasks work:

- **Auto-generated from strategy** — When a citation strategy or opportunity brief is ready, tasks can be created directly from it. The brief, target keyword, acceptance criteria, and effort estimate come pre-filled.
- **Manual creation** — Any team member can also create tasks from scratch and attach them to a keyword.
- **Task types** — Content (writing, restructuring, expanding), Technical (schema, indexing, internal links), Off-page (outreach, citation building)
- **Acceptance criteria** — Every task has a checklist of specific conditions that must be met before it can be marked complete. Vague "done" is not an option.
- **Status workflow** — To Do → In Progress → Done (or Skip), with timestamps at each stage
- **Outcome verification** — When a task is marked done, the platform automatically re-checks the keyword's ranking and AI citation status and labels the result: ✓ Verified (it worked), ↓ Regressed (something went wrong), or Neutral (no change yet)
- **Context alerts** — If the underlying SERP or AI answer changes significantly while a task is still open, the task gets flagged so your team re-evaluates before closing it

**Why it matters:** Most agencies track SEO work in a separate project tool with no connection to the actual data. In VSI, the task *is* the data — it was created from a specific gap, it closes when specific criteria are met, and the platform tells you whether the fix worked.

## Capability 6 — VSI Assistant

**What you get:** An AI assistant built into every page of the platform that answers questions about your actual data — not generic SEO advice.

### What the Assistant knows:

- Your live keyword rankings and AI citation status for the current client
- Which competitors are being cited and for which keywords
- What tasks are open and what their current status is
- The AI answers and citations from the most recent scan

### What the Assistant can do:

- Answer questions like "What's the biggest opportunity right now?" or "Why aren't we cited for this keyword?"
- Explain the gap classifications in plain language for a client call
- Draft strategic recommendations based on the real data in the account
- Operate at three scopes: a specific keyword, a specific client, or across all clients

### What the Assistant won't do:

- Invent rankings that aren't in the data
- Reference competitors that weren't found in your actual scans
- Give generic SEO advice disconnected from your specific situation

**Why it matters:** Instead of switching between the dashboard and a separate AI tool, your strategist asks a question and gets an answer grounded in the exact data already in the system.
