## 2. The problem

Search stopped being a list. It's now a paragraph with three citations, and your buyer is reading that paragraph before they ever see a result page. The rank trackers your team still pays for don't read that paragraph — they count positions on a results page that fewer people scroll to.

So three things break at once. Traffic drops with no ranking explanation. Competitors you've never heard of show up cited in your category. And the weekly client report you've been sending for six years quietly stops matching what the client experiences when they search for themselves.

The honest answer your team needs — *which buyer questions surface our brand inside the AI answer, and which ones hand the conversation to someone else* — can't be screenshot one query at a time. It has to be audited at scale, classified by gap type, and turned into shippable work. That's the gap VSI was built to close.

## 3. The promise — four value propositions

**We show you exactly where competitors are winning citations.** By reading the live AI answer for every keyword you track and extracting every cited domain, anchor, and entity in the response.

**We tell you the specific reason AI skipped your page.** By auditing the missing schema, entity coverage, freshness signals, and authority markers against the pages that did get quoted.

**We hand your team a ready-to-ship work order, not a PDF report.** Each gap becomes a scoped task — content brief, schema patch, or internal link — with the keyword, page, and predicted lift attached.

**We verify the citation actually returned after the work shipped.** By re-running the same AI queries on a schedule and proving — with screenshots and source lists — that your brand is now inside the answer.

## 4. Feature catalogue

### Pillar 1 — Visibility tracking
- **SERP rank tracking** — Brand position across keywords and locations, capturing top 10 results, titles, URLs, and SERP features in a single run.
- **AI Mode monitoring** — Whether the search engine returns an AI answer for each keyword, and whether your brand appears in or is cited within it.
- **AI Overview tracking** — Optional capture of the newer AI Overview surface, distinct from AI Mode, for dual-engine visibility.
- **Citation capture** — Rich metadata on every domain cited in an AI answer: URL, position, source name. The competitive set your brand is actually fighting.
- **Conversational AI ranking** — Branded searches run through conversational AI to capture mention frequency, positioning, and cited URLs.
- **Gap classification** — Every keyword auto-labelled as Winning, Mentioned, Invisible, or Not capturing.
- **Scheduled pipeline runs** — Recurring scans so the dataset stays current without manual re-runs.

### Pillar 2 — AI diagnosis
- **Opportunity brief** — A prioritised recommendation per keyword, grounded in the latest snapshot: ranking gap, AI-answer presence, dominant competitors, recommended angle.
- **Topic relevance detection** — Flags when the AI answer is off-topic for your industry, so the recommendation pivots to disambiguation instead of citation injection.
- **Confidence scoring** — Each brief carries a confidence level based on data freshness, SERP completeness, and answer richness.
- **Organic context** — Every diagnosis includes the top 10 organic results so recommendations account for both surfaces.

### Pillar 3 — Citation strategy
- **Competitive content extraction** — Scrapes the pages cited in the AI answer to extract topic patterns, structure, and source authority.
- **Citation gap analysis** — Surfaces what competitors are cited for and your brand isn't: depth, length, format, authoritative sources.
- **Action roadmap** — Ranked, executable steps with effort and impact estimates: content angles, link targets, schema patches.
- **Client page audit** — Your ranked page benchmarked against cited competitors with specific changes that improve citation likelihood.
- **Strategic patterns** — Recurring themes across the competitive set so strategy addresses the pattern, not the outlier.

### Pillar 4 — Execution & task tracker
- **Task creation and import** — Manual or auto-imported from strategy reports, with group, assignee, effort, impact, and acceptance criteria.
- **Outcome verification** — When a task is marked done, the platform re-checks whether AI presence or rank actually improved and labels the task verified, regressed, or neutral.
- **Keyword-scoped snapshot** — Each task captures the visibility state at creation, so progress is measurable without re-running the pipeline.
- **Status workflow** — To Do → In Progress → Done (or Skip), with live acceptance-criteria checklist tracking.
- **Effort and impact estimates** — Small/medium/large effort, low/medium/high impact, so execution teams prioritise quick wins.

### Pillar 5 — Reports & sharing

## 5. User workflows

### Workflow 1 — Agency owner, Monday portfolio review
**Goal:** Read what changed over the weekend and route the week's work in under ten minutes.

1. Land on the portfolio overview: every client tile with week-over-week visibility delta, gap movement, and unresolved-task counts.
2. Sort by largest negative swing in the last 7 days; open the worst tile first.
3. Read the auto-generated weekend summary at the top of the client dashboard — what dropped, what gained, what's new in citations.
4. Drill into the keyword cluster driving the dip; the keyword detail page shows ranking history, citation share, and a written diagnosis.
5. Skim the suggested actions panel; accept the three that match priorities, dismiss the rest.
6. Convert accepted actions into tickets with one click — brief, target keyword, acceptance criteria, and due date pre-filled.
7. Assign each ticket from the team dropdown; assignees are notified.
8. Mark the client "reviewed this week" and move to the next red tile.

**Magic moment:** The weekend summary explains *why* rankings moved before anyone has to ask.

### Workflow 2 — SEO strategist, keyword diagnosis
**Goal:** Take a single underperforming keyword from symptom to a writer-ready task list.

1. Open the client, filter the keyword table to "declining" plus "high commercial intent."
2. Click into the top offender; the detail view loads ranking trend, SERP features, and AI-answer citation share side by side.
3. Read the auto-generated brief: intent classification, current ranking page, gaps versus the top three results, recommended angle.
4. Open the citation analysis tab to see which sources the AI answer is quoting and where the client could realistically earn a mention.
5. Compare the client's existing page against the gap list — what's missing, thin, or off-intent.
6. Click "generate task list"; the system breaks the fix into discrete tickets — rewrite intro, add comparison table, add schema, build two internal links, pitch one citation source.
7. Tighten acceptance criteria so a writer cannot mark done without hitting the specific gaps.
8. Assign writing tickets to a copywriter, technical tickets to the publisher, add a handoff note.
9. Mark the keyword "in remediation"; a progress badge appears on the client dashboard.

**Magic moment:** Citation analysis names the exact pages they need to outrank in AI answers — not a vague "improve content" suggestion.

### Workflow 3 — Copywriter, picking up assigned work
**Goal:** Ship a draft that passes acceptance criteria the first time.

1. Land on "my tasks," filtered to "ready to start," sorted by due date.
2. Open the top ticket; brief, target keyword, word count, internal-link targets, and acceptance checklist are all attached.
3. Read the brief — intent, audience, the strategist's chosen angle, the gap list the draft must close.
4. Click "generate outline" for a section scaffold tied to the gap list; edit and reorder as needed.
5. Move into the draft editor; brief, outline, and acceptance criteria stay pinned in a side rail.
6. Write; the editor flags missing internal links, missing keyword variants, and unchecked criteria in real time.
7. Tick off each acceptance criterion as it's satisfied; anything red blocks submission.
8. Submit; the ticket moves to the publisher's queue, the strategist is notified.

**Magic moment:** The side rail makes the brief feel like a co-writer instead of a PDF to remember.

### Workflow 4 — Web publisher, post-publish checklist
**Goal:** Take the draft live and confirm it's discoverable, structured, and connected.

1. Open the ticket from "ready to publish"; the draft, target URL, and checklist are pre-loaded.
2. Push to the live site and paste the published URL; the checklist activates against that URL.
3. Run the schema check — what's there, what's missing for the intent type, copy-paste block for the gap.
4. Run the internal-link check — suggested source pages on the same site that should link to the new one, with anchor-text recommendations; add them.
5. Run the indexing check — submit for indexing, confirm crawlable, canonical-clean, not blocked.
6. Run the citation-readiness check — answer-shaped passages, headings, and entity markup that make the page quotable by AI.
7. Tick each item; anything failing stays red and blocks closing the ticket.
8. Mark complete; the keyword flips from "in remediation" to "shipped, monitoring."

**Magic moment:** The internal-link panel surfaces the exact existing pages that should link to the new one — no manual site crawl.

### Workflow 5 — Agency owner, client review meeting
**Goal:** Walk a client through the week in a branded, shareable view.

1. Open the client dashboard and click "generate weekly report."
2. The builder pulls visibility movement, ranking wins and losses, citation share change, completed work, and the opportunity backlog — auto-narrated.
3. Apply the agency branding preset; edit any narration line that needs softening or sharpening.
4. Publish for a shareable link; send to the client before the call.
5. On the call, open the live presentation view — same content, full-screen, section by section.
6. Walk wins first: keywords that moved up, new citations earned, traffic gained.
7. Walk losses honestly: what dropped, the diagnosed cause, the tickets already in flight to fix it.
8. Walk opportunities: ranked backlog with effort, intent, and expected lift.
9. Close by converting selected opportunities to tickets in one click — they appear in the team queue before the call ends.

**Magic moment:** The client sees losses framed alongside the already-assigned fix — accountability without defensiveness.

## 9. Objection-handling FAQs

**"Isn't this just what our existing SEO suite already does?"**
The big suites track positions on the classic results page. They don't read the AI answer itself, don't extract the cited sources, and don't tell you which entity or schema gap caused the omission. VSI is built for the surface that's eating your clicks, not the one your reports still measure. Run both side by side for a week — the delta is the point.

**"Will my team need weeks of training?"**
No. The workflow is the same one your strategists already run mentally — audit, plan, ship, verify — just consolidated into one screen. New users complete their first real client audit on day one. We onboard the whole team in a single 45-minute session, recorded, with templated playbooks for the five most common gap types.

**"Does this work for Arabic, Urdu, and other non-Latin markets?"**
Yes — that's why we were built in the GCC. We track AI answers in Arabic and English in parallel, handle right-to-left content audits, and flag entity mismatches between language variants of the same brand. Most Western-built tools treat these markets as an afterthought. We treat them as the home market.

**"How do I know the data is real and not modelled?"**
Every citation we report comes from a live AI response we captured, with a timestamped screenshot and the full source list attached to the record. Nothing is inferred from rank position or estimated from traffic curves. If we say a competitor was cited, you can open the exact answer and see it.

**"Do I have to install a browser extension or change my stack?"**
No extension, no DNS change, no tag on the site. You add a domain and the keywords you care about, and the platform runs the audits in the cloud. The only thing your team installs is the habit of checking the dashboard before the weekly client call.
